Services that move your business forward

No jargon, no endless workshops that lead nowhere. Just practical support, delivered by people who understand what it takes to build a business in Rwanda.

Business Coaching

One on one coaching sessions where we look at your actual business, spot what is holding it back and agree on clear next steps you can act on immediately.

Training

Group trainings on record keeping, pricing, marketing, customer care and digital tools. Short, focused and built around real examples from our members.

Business Advisory

Guidance on formalization, taxes, financing options and growth strategy. We tell you what you need to hear, not just what you want to hear.

Mentorship

We match you with experienced entrepreneurs who have faced the same challenges you face now, and who are willing to share what actually worked.

Market Linkages

The service our members talk about most. We connect your products to buyers in local markets, the EAC region and AfCFTA opportunities across Africa.

B2B Networking

Curated business to business meetings where you sit across from suppliers, distributors and partners who are genuinely relevant to your work.

Consultancy

Deeper engagements for organizations and growing businesses: market studies, program design, value chain analysis and sector research.

Event Organization

From training days to sector forums, we plan and run events that bring the right people into the same room and leave them with something useful.

Exhibitions

We organize our own exhibitions and take members to national and regional ones, so your products get seen, tested and bought.

Investment & Export Readiness

Standards, packaging, certification, documentation and pitch preparation. Everything your business needs before serious buyers and investors say yes.

Step by step support at your pace
How We Work

Simple process, honest support

  • 1. We listen first. Every engagement starts with understanding your business, your market and your goals.
  • 2. We build a plan together. You get a clear picture of the services that fit your stage, not a one size fits all package.
  • 3. We open doors. Coaching and training are paired with real introductions to markets, buyers and partners.
  • 4. We stay with you. Growth takes time. We keep checking in, adjusting and celebrating progress with you.
Request a Service

Not sure which service you need?

That is completely normal. Send us a message describing where your business is right now, and we will point you in the right direction, free of charge.
