Services that move your business forward

No jargon, no endless workshops that lead nowhere. Just practical support, delivered by people who understand what it takes to build a business in Rwanda.

Business Coaching

One on one coaching sessions where we look at your actual business, spot what is holding it back and agree on clear next steps you can act on immediately.

Training

Group trainings on record keeping, pricing, marketing, customer care and digital tools. Short, focused and built around real examples from our members.

Business Advisory

Guidance on formalization, taxes, financing options and growth strategy. We tell you what you need to hear, not just what you want to hear.

Mentorship

We match you with experienced entrepreneurs who have faced the same challenges you face now, and who are willing to share what actually worked.

Market Linkages

The service our members talk about most. We connect your products to buyers in local markets, the EAC region and AfCFTA opportunities across Africa.

B2B Networking

Curated business to business meetings where you sit across from suppliers, distributors and partners who are genuinely relevant to your work.

Consultancy

Deeper engagements for organizations and growing businesses: market studies, program design, value chain analysis and sector research.

Event Organization

From training days to sector forums, we plan and run events that bring the right people into the same room and leave them with something useful.

Exhibitions

We organize our own exhibitions and take members to national and regional ones, so your products get seen, tested and bought.

Investment & Export Readiness

Standards, packaging, certification, documentation and pitch preparation. Everything your business needs before serious buyers and investors say yes.

Learning Lab

Practical guides you can actually use

Short answers to the questions our members ask most. Tap a topic to read it. For the exact current requirements, always confirm with the official institution linked in Access to Information below.

Getting your produce ready for export Agribusiness

Export buyers care about consistency: the same quality, size and packaging in every batch. Start by talking to other farmers or a cooperative near you about aggregating your harvest, since buyers usually need volumes a single small farm cannot supply alone. The National Agricultural Export Development Board (NAEB) sets the current requirements and certification steps for exporting Rwandan agricultural products. Talk to our team first and we will help you understand what applies to your specific crop before you approach them.

Starting or strengthening a cooperative Agribusiness

A well run cooperative gives small farmers real bargaining power: shared transport, shared storage, and one voice at the negotiating table. Cooperatives are formed and officially recognized through the Rwanda Cooperatives Agency (RCA). If you already farm alongside others informally, we can help you understand the steps to formalize and the basic governance a healthy cooperative needs.

Basics of adding value to what you grow Agribusiness

Selling raw produce usually earns less than selling it processed, for example dried fruit instead of fresh fruit, or packaged flour instead of raw grain. Before investing in processing equipment, it helps to understand the food safety and quality standards that apply, which are set by the Rwanda Standards Board and the Rwanda Food and Drugs Authority. Talk to our team about which standards matter for your specific product.

Formalizing your creative business Creative Industry

Many creative entrepreneurs sell for years before registering formally, and it usually costs them opportunities: banks, serious buyers and exhibition organizers tend to prefer working with a registered business. Business registration and a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) are handled through the Rwanda Development Board (RDB) and the Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA). We can walk you through what to expect and answer your questions before you go.

Protecting your work: the basics of intellectual property Creative Industry

If you design, write, compose or create original work, you may be able to protect it through copyright or trademark registration, which makes it harder for others to copy your brand or your designs without permission. Intellectual property registration in Rwanda is handled through RDB. It is worth understanding this early, especially before you exhibit your work publicly or start selling under a brand name.

Getting ready for your first exhibition Creative Industry

An exhibition is often the first time buyers see your work in person, so preparation matters more than most people expect: a clear price list, business cards, a short pitch you can say comfortably, and enough stock to actually sell. RYCP organizes exhibitions and prepares members beforehand. Ask us about our next exhibition and how we help members get ready.

Access to Information

Straight to the official source

When you need the exact, current answer, go directly to the institution responsible. These are the ones our members contact most, grouped by what they help with.

Business Registration & Tax

Rwanda Development Board (RDB)

Register your business, apply for licenses, and start the process for trademark or copyright registration. RDB is the first stop for most formal business questions in Rwanda.

Visit rdb.rw

Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA)

Get your Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), understand your tax obligations, and file as your business grows.

Visit rra.gov.rw

Agriculture & Export

National Agricultural Export Development Board (NAEB)

The official source for agricultural export requirements, certification and market information for Rwandan farmers and agribusinesses.

Visit naeb.gov.rw

Rwanda Cooperatives Agency (RCA)

Guidance on forming, registering and governing a farmer or producer cooperative.

Visit rca.gov.rw

Standards & Quality

Rwanda Standards Board (RSB)

Quality standards and certification for products, useful before you scale up processing or packaging.

Visit rsb.gov.rw

Rwanda Food and Drugs Authority

Safety compliance for food, drinks and cosmetic products before they reach the market.

Visit rwandafda.gov.rw

Business Support & Trade

Private Sector Federation (PSF)

Advocacy and business support for private sector entrepreneurs. RYCP works with PSF through ACTR.

Visit psf.org.rw

Ministry of Trade and Industry

National trade policy, export facilitation and industry development.

Visit minicom.gov.rw
