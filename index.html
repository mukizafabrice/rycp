Welcome to RYCP

A community built by young people, for young people

RYCP Rwanda Community stands for Rwanda Rural and Urban Youth Competitiveness Promoters. We are a registered social enterprise based at Maison des Jeunes in Kimisagara, Kigali, and we exist for one simple reason: too many talented young Rwandans have great business ideas but struggle to access markets, finance and the right guidance.

We close that gap. Through coaching, training, market linkages and honest advice, we help entrepreneurs in both rural and urban areas move from surviving to competing, at home and beyond our borders.

  • Registered and accountable. A Community Based Company registered under RDB.
  • Inclusive by design. We serve youth, women, persons with disabilities, farmers and creatives.
  • Practical, not theoretical. Real market linkages, real exhibitions, real results.

A story from our community

From a small plot to a steady market

In 2024 we met a group of horticulture farmers who grew good produce but sold it at whatever price the nearest middleman offered. Their harvests were solid. Their incomes were not.

We worked with them on record keeping, pricing and quality standards, then connected them directly to buyers through our market linkage program. Today, 60 of the farmers we support sell to reliable markets at fair prices, and many are reinvesting in their farms for the first time.

"Before, we farmed and hoped. Now we farm with a plan, because we know where our produce is going and what it is worth."
JB
Jean Bosco Horticulture farmer, RYCP market linkage program

What We Do

Support that meets you where you are

Whether you are testing your first idea or preparing your products for export, we have a service designed for your stage of growth.

Coaching & Training

Hands-on business coaching and practical training that build the skills you actually use to run and grow an enterprise.

Market Linkages

We connect your products and services to buyers in local markets, across East Africa and through AfCFTA opportunities.

Mentorship & B2B Networking

Learn from entrepreneurs who have walked the road before you, and meet partners who can open the next door.

Business Advisory

Honest, practical advice on formalization, financing, pricing and strategy from people who understand the Rwandan market.

Exhibitions & Events

We organize and join national and regional exhibitions so your products get in front of the right customers.

Investment & Export Readiness

Get your business ready for serious buyers and investors, with support on standards, packaging and documentation.
