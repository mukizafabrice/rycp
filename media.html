Aggregation center concept gains momentum

Our proposal for regional aggregation centers is moving forward. The centers would help horticulture farmers consolidate produce, hit quality standards and negotiate better prices together.

New training cycle kicks off in Kimisagara

Another group of entrepreneurs started our business skills training at Maison des Jeunes, covering record keeping, pricing and digital marketing basics.

Policy recommendations reach decision makers

Our 27 page policy document on employment creation and entrepreneurship challenges was submitted, with practical recommendations on market access for SMEs.
