Common Questions

Before you join

Who can become a member?

Membership is open to youth, women, persons with disabilities, small and medium micro entrepreneurs, farmers and creative industry practitioners across Rwanda. If you run a business or are seriously working on starting one, you are welcome.

How do I pay the membership fee?

After you register online, our team confirms your details and shares payment instructions. You can pay by MoMoPay or bank transfer. Payment details are also listed on our contact page.

Can I upgrade my membership later?

Yes, at any time. Many members start with Bronze while they build a local base, then move to Silver or Gold when they are ready for regional and international markets. You only pay the difference for the remaining period.

What happens after I register?

We contact you within two working days to confirm your information and payment. Once confirmed, you get a welcome session where we learn about your business and match you with the right services, mentors and cluster.

Still thinking it over?

Come visit us at Maison des Jeunes in Kimisagara, or send us a message. We would rather answer your questions honestly than rush you into joining.
