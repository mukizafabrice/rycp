Our business clusters

We organize our work around two sectors where young Rwandans are already building real value: agribusiness and the creative industry. Focus lets us go deep instead of spreading thin.

ISHU Initiatives our flagship program
Flagship Program

ISHU: Igire Smart, Hanga Ukore

Both clusters sit under ISHU Initiatives, our flagship entrepreneurship and economic empowerment program. The name carries the message: be smart about your future, create and get to work.

ISHU covers entrepreneurship promotion, business incubation and mentorship, capacity building, market linkage, innovation and technology adoption, agribusiness development, creative industry development and employment creation.

The goal behind all of it is simple. We want young people to become job creators, not just job seekers.

Cluster 01

Agribusiness

Agriculture feeds Rwanda and employs most of its people, yet too many farmers still work hard for too little. We help them farm smarter, add value and sell better.

Agriculture

Direct support for crop and livestock farmers to grow smarter, more resilient and more profitable operations.

Agriculture Extension Services

Hands-on extension support that helps farmers apply the right techniques, inputs and timing for stronger yields.

Post Harvest

Reducing losses after harvest through proper handling, storage and processing before produce reaches the market.

Aggregation Center

Regional aggregation centers that bring smallholder harvests together for better quality control and bargaining power.

Online Market Linkages

Connecting farmers to buyers through digital platforms that cut out unfair middlemen and unlock fair prices.

School Field Farmers

Training young people through school based demonstration farms that build practical agribusiness skills early.

Cluster 02

Creative Industry

Rwanda's creative economy is full of talent that deserves real income. We support creators to formalize, protect their work and reach paying audiences and buyers.

ICT & Innovation (AI)

Digital products, software services and artificial intelligence powered tools solving local problems.

Fashion & Design

Designers and tailors building brands, from made in Rwanda clothing to accessories.

Arts & Crafts

Creative artisans producing beadwork, sculpture and handmade pieces with stories worth telling.

Content Creation, Media & Marketing

Content creators, photographers and marketers building brands and audiences on social media.

Pyrography Arts

Artists turning wood burning techniques into decorative pieces ready for sale.

Knitting Machine

Entrepreneurs using knitting machines to produce garments and textiles at scale.

Crochet

Skilled crocheters creating handmade fashion pieces, home decor and accessories.

Mat Making

Artisans weaving mats and woven home products for local and export markets.
