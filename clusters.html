Our business clusters

We organize our work around two sectors where young Rwandans are already building real value: agribusiness and the creative industry. Focus lets us go deep instead of spreading thin.

ISHU Initiatives our flagship program
Flagship Program

ISHU: Igire Smart, Hanga Ukore

Both clusters sit under ISHU Initiatives, our flagship entrepreneurship and economic empowerment program. The name carries the message: be smart about your future, create and get to work.

ISHU covers entrepreneurship promotion, business incubation and mentorship, capacity building, market linkage, innovation and technology adoption, agribusiness development, creative industry development and employment creation.

The goal behind all of it is simple. We want young people to become job creators, not just job seekers.

Cluster 01

Agribusiness

Agriculture feeds Rwanda and employs most of its people, yet too many farmers still work hard for too little. We help them farm smarter, add value and sell better.

Agriculture

Support for crop farmers on climate smart practices, quality standards and farmer aggregation that gives smallholders bargaining power.

Livestock

Helping livestock keepers improve productivity, animal health practices and access to processing and market channels.

Agro-processing

Turning raw harvests into packaged, branded products that keep more value in the hands of the producer.

Agritech

Encouraging digital tools for farm records, weather information, market prices and traceability, adapted to how farmers actually work.

Cooperatives

Strengthening farmer cooperatives with good governance, financial discipline and collective marketing skills.

Export Readiness

Preparing agribusinesses for regional and international markets through standards, certification and packaging support.

Cluster 02

Creative Industry

Rwanda's creative economy is full of talent that deserves real income. We support creators to formalize, protect their work and reach paying audiences and buyers.

ICT & Innovation

Digital products, software services and tech enabled startups solving local problems.

Fashion

Designers and tailors building brands, from made in Rwanda clothing to accessories.

Arts & Crafts

Crochet, beadwork, pyrography and handmade crafts with stories worth telling and prices worth paying.

Media & Design

Photographers, videographers and designers turning creative skill into steady client work.

Music

Musicians and producers learning the business side of their art, from rights to revenue.

Theatre

Performers and cultural entrepreneurs bringing stories to stages and audiences.

IP Awareness

Helping creators understand and protect the intellectual property behind their work.

Policy Advocacy

Speaking up for creatives in the rooms where decisions about the sector are made.

Which cluster fits your business?

Whether you grow it, make it, design it or perform it, there is a place for you in our community.
